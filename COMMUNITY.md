# Community Principles

Altru Browser exists under the idea **Code for Humanity**: technology should serve people rather than require people to serve the technology.

Community participation should be technically rigorous and respectful.

- Critique code, evidence, assumptions, and architecture rather than people.
- Make claims proportionate to evidence.
- Disclose limitations and conflicts of interest.
- Do not submit secrets, personal data, credentials, or customer material.
- Do not introduce hidden telemetry or deceptive behavior.
- Respect licenses, attribution, and upstream contribution rules.

Maintainers may close or reject contributions that weaken safety boundaries, misrepresent project maturity, or create avoidable legal/security risk.
