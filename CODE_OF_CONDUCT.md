# Code of Conduct

Altru Browser is built under the principle **Code for Humanity**.

We expect participation to be respectful, technically rigorous and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- discuss code, evidence and ideas without attacking people;
- be precise about what is known, measured or speculative;
- welcome good-faith questions and corrections;
- respect privacy, attribution, licenses and security boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats or deliberate disruption.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, malicious disclosure of private information, deliberate security exploitation against community members, or repeated bad-faith disruption is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, lock or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from project spaces.

For sensitive conduct concerns, use a private contact route rather than a public issue.
