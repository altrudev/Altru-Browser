## Approved product-shell baseline

The implemented browser shell remains **page-first** with three HIO states:

- **Focus** — content dominates; chrome recedes.
- **Navigate** — a very thin top bar exposes back, forward, URL/search, and minimal browser controls.
- **Inspect** — Page Intelligence/evidence appears temporarily and on demand.

Concept-art capability panels or navigation surfaces do not automatically authorize permanent chrome in the shipping shell.

## Visual language

Ukrainian visual identity is expressed through the canonical woven mark and restrained structural details. Use sky-blue, wheat-gold, restrained red, white and dark navy with natural materials/light. Vyshyvanka-inspired geometry should be subtle: separators, contours, notches, transition motifs, or edge details—not decorative overload.

The product should feel realistic, calm, technically advanced and buildable rather than fantastical.

## Product communication

Product imagery should make clear why Altru Browser is not a browser clone. Approved explanatory themes include:

- independent native engine;
- local-first privacy and minimal permissions;
- adaptive resource use;
- ACIR / Capability Compiler;
- Altru Companion as a personal adaptive automation layer;
- Research / Create / Solve / Local / Assist / Spaces workflows;
- governed evolution through DDC / Frequency;
- evidence and inspectability;
- human-centered rather than ad-centered design.

Comparisons with other browsers must distinguish design goals and verified architecture from unverified performance or privacy claims about competitors.

## Platform language

Target operating systems:

- Windows
- macOS
- Linux
- Android
- iOS

Current engineering evidence, not concept art, determines which targets may be called runtime-supported or production-ready. Refer to `docs/PLATFORM-MATRIX.md` for the live verification state.

A PWA must **never** be listed as an operating system. If a PWA build is discussed, label it only as the **portable version**.

## Asset rule

No screenshot, social image, icon, wordmark, or mockup is canonical merely because it exists under `assets/`.

An asset is official only when it is explicitly approved and referenced by this document or another governed brand manifest. The canonical logo master above must be used without reinterpretation.
